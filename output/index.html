This is a working tool, not a brochure. Wherever you are in it you can type, drop a pin on the model, vote for an option, resize a space, mark up a plan, rank what matters, say what must stay or stand at a building in the model, and the tool records what it was about.

What happens to what you add

Everything you add stays on this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere. The design team collects it from the device at the end of the session and adds it to the record; until then you can remove it all here. No name or contact is asked for; please don't add other people's. An export carries a random id for this phone, not your name, so the team can tell phones apart; Remove all resets it.
